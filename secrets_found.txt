TOOL LIMITATIONS OBSERVED:
1. TruffleHog v2 (entropy-based detection) flagged only 1 of 6
   secrets (AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY) in the initial scan. This is a
   known limitation of entropy-only detection on shorter or
   lower-randomness strings.
2. grep required the -i (case-insensitive) flag to catch uppercase
   variable names such as AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID and FLUTTERWAVE_SECRET.
   The lab's original case-sensitive pattern missed 4 of 6 secrets.
3. Even with -i, DATABASE_PASS was missed on the first pass because
   the pattern only included "password"/"passwd", not the short
   form "pass". Adding "pass" to the regex closed this gap, though
   it broadens the pattern (risk of false positives on words like
   "passage" or "surpass" in a larger codebase).

CONCLUSION: No single detection method is complete. Entropy-based
tools and regex/pattern-based tools each have blind spots and
should be combined, alongside manual review, for reliable secrets
detection.
